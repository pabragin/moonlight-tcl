<p>A build of Moonlight (via the Artemis fork) tuned for the MediaTek chip in <b>TCL Google TVs on Android 14 firmware</b>: C8K, C7K, C6K, QM6K, QM8K and similar. It streams games and the desktop from <a href="https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine">Sunshine</a> or <a href="https://github.com/ClassicOldSong/Apollo">Apollo</a> on your PC.</p>

<p><b>The TV never freezes or reboots.</b> TCL's Android 14 firmware crashes its input service when an app sends gamepad rumble: the TV shows the boot animation and every app restarts. Moonlight TCL sends rumble through the Bluetooth stack instead, so the pad shakes and the TV stays up. On older firmware that freezes when you leave the stream, it keeps a second layer above the video by default.</p>

<p><b>Every frame reaches the screen.</b> Same TV, same 4K60 HDR stream at 100 Mbps, counted at the TV's compositor: the official Artemis put all 60 frames on screen in 0 of 48 samples, Moonlight TCL in 44 of 48, with a third less CPU. Decoded frames go to the screen straight from the decoder's callback, and sound leaves through native AAudio.</p>

<p><b>Small and quick.</b> A 2 MB APK with all 32 languages, 32 MB of RAM on the main screen. The inherited crashes are fixed, and the settings that could not change anything on a TV are gone: 45 settings instead of 85.</p>

<p><b>Made for the TV and the pad:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>PCs as chips, apps as posters, an action panel on Menu or Y, two-pane settings</li>
<li>An on-screen keyboard with dictation, driven by the gamepad or the remote</li>
<li>A card when a pad joins, runs low or goes on the charger</li>
<li>Defaults set for a 4K TV: 3840×2160, 60 FPS, HEVC, HDR, 100 Mbps</li>
<li><i>moonlight-tcl://</i> links to start a stream from Home Assistant</li>
</ul>

<p>It installs as its own app (com.limelight.tcl) next to Moonlight or Artemis; pairings and settings are per app.</p>

<p><b>Requirements:</b> a TCL Google TV on Android 14 (armeabi-v7a), and Sunshine or Apollo on the PC. HDR needs a GPU that encodes HEVC Main 10. Use Ethernet through a USB gigabit adapter: the TV's own port is 100 Mbps, and Wi-Fi adds latency and jitter.</p>

<p>If the TV freezes or goes black when you leave the stream, update its firmware: TCL fixed that in V655 (T653T01 platform) and V339 (North American 0012T02).</p>
